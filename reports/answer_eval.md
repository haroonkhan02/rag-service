# Answer evaluation

Answers come from the service's own `ask` path with caches off, generated by `mlx-community/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-4bit` (local, greedy decoding) from the top 5 passages. 66 questions: the evaluation set of robotics-docs-rag, 61 answerable and 5 that the corpus cannot answer.

An answer to an answerable question is correct when the judge (`qwen7b`) finds the reference answer's key facts in it; an abstention there is wrong. An unanswerable question is correct when the service abstains. Answers from Claude are not evaluated here: that requires an Anthropic API key.

## Can the judge be trusted?

Control items have a known verdict: the reference answer graded against itself (should be correct), another question's reference answer, and the service's "cannot answer" sentence (both should be incorrect). They are easy cases: they show that the judge is not broken, not that it is right on hard ones.

| Judge | Reference vs itself | Another question's answer | Abstention sentence | Verdicts without a tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| qwen7b | 61/61 | 61/61 | 61/61 | 0 |
| llama3b | 59/61 | 56/61 | 47/61 | 8 |

Correct on answerable questions according to each judge:

| Retrieval | qwen7b | llama3b | Judges agree |
|---|---|---|---|
| bm25 | 73.8 % (45/61) | 86.9 % (53/61) | 44/56 answers |
| hybrid_rerank | 82.0 % (50/61) | 90.2 % (55/61) | 53/58 answers |

Agreement is counted over the answers both judges graded (abstentions are scored without a judge).
